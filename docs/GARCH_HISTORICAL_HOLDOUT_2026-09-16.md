# TSLA Gaussian GARCH historical holdout — exploratory check (16 September 2026)

**This is a short, retrospective pilot, not validation of the original five-year model, a trading result, or a claim that GARCH reliably forecasts TSLA.** Data are 133 daily closing prices (132 log returns) spanning 2024-05-22 to 2024-11-29, transcribed to cents from the [public historical CSV](https://github.com/FarhanAli97/Tesla-TSLA-Stock-Data-2010-to-December-2024/blob/main/Tesla%20%28TSLA%29%20From%202010%20To%20Dec-2024.csv), whose Git blob SHA at the audit was `8b994c2ad47919d32905d60c942f8de6489d5b07`. The local audit bundle preserves the excerpt, 1-step predictions and complete evaluation script; the whole original CSV has not been independently retrieved into this environment. Transcription introduces a checkable data-quality risk.

Fit a **restricted** Gaussian GARCH(1,1) on the first 80 returns, ending 2024-09-17, with a train-only mean and `omega=(1-alpha-beta)*training_variance`; multistart SLSQP with nonnegative coefficients and `alpha+beta<0.999`. Freeze parameters and form forecasts of each next day's conditional variance *before observing that day's return*. Baselines: fixed training variance, EWMA with decay 0.94. Evaluate 52 later returns (2024-09-18 to 2024-11-29) using QLIKE `log(h)+(r-mu)^2/h` (lower is better). This restricted fit differs from the three-free-parameter `estimate_garch` implementation in `project5_GARCH.py`; **do not ascribe this pilot's parameters to that routine**.

| Model | Average out-of-sample QLIKE | Mean forecast daily volatility |
|---|---:|---:|
| Restricted GARCH | -4.94886 | 0.03830 |
| Constant train variance | -4.98703 | 0.03776 |
| EWMA (0.94) | -4.72972 | 0.04337 |

The fitted `alpha=0.08244`, `beta≈0` and the paired QLIKE difference `GARCH − constant = +0.03818` have a circular moving-block (five-day blocks, 3,000 resamples) 95% interval `[-0.02977, 0.12258]`, including zero. **There is no demonstrated GARCH advantage over the constant baseline.** Across train/test splits of 60/72, 80/52 and 100/32 returns, the GARCH-minus-constant QLIKE differences were respectively +0.08671, +0.03818 and approximately zero, while fitted coefficients shifted markedly (beta 0.840, ~0, ~0). Results are sensitive to the short training period.

Limitations: 2024 historical closes only, approximate manual transcription, a single security and three overlapping splits, no corporate-action audit or adjusted prices, no overnight/intraday realized variance, squared returns are a noisy volatility proxy, 52 observations are too few for reliable 5% VaR coverage analysis, and bootstrap intervals are exploratory. This is not a fully out-of-sample five-year benchmark, and it says nothing about 2026 market conditions. Longer verified adjusted-close records, rolling multi-year train/test windows and heavy-tailed alternatives are necessary before quantitative forecasting claims.
